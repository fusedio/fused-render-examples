Overture vs Philadelphia

Scores seven Overture Maps releases against all 546,070 of Philadelphia's official building footprints. The first run downloads both datasets and runs the conflation locally in DuckDB — about 1.7 GB of disk and 45–60 minutes, once. After that the app is instant and offline.
